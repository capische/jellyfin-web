Optional Field Marker

On a phone the settings fields' floating label is one line and is cut with an ellipsis, so (Optional), the last thing in it, is what disappears. A is today; B moves the word to the line under the field, where text wraps; C replaces it with an icon.

Recommendation: B. The word stays a word: no legend to learn, nothing to hover on a phone or a TV, and it can never be cut.
