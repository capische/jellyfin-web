Season Total Download

Season page: the season's total download, in the header

One indicator in the header's action row, right after the download cloud: the season's ring (the row-size download mark with the total arc and the liveliest glyph) and, beside it, the figure for the whole season — episodes done of episodes on their way, the total percent and the time left. It appears while any episode of the season is queued, downloading or importing, and is gone when nothing is.

Laptop · five states

Order in the row: Play · Played · Get a Release (cloud) · season total · Favourite · More. The total is an indicator, not a button: no hover, no focus stop, the tooltip carries the breakdown. The cloud keeps its job (get a release for the missing episodes) whether or not something downloads.

Series page · the same indicator, total over every season (added 2026-10-24)

Same component, same place (after the cloud where the page has one, otherwise before Favourite), same figure; M and N count every season's episodes the queue has rows for, the tooltip breaks the total down by season. The series card already shows this aggregate, so the page and the card agree.

Phone 360 (90 %) · the figure drops under the row

The header row is centred and icon-only on the phone, so the ring joins it and the figure becomes one centred line under the row. Nothing is cut.

TV 125 % · the same row; focus walks Play → Played → cloud → Favourite → More and skips the total

No plugin field is needed: the queue rows already carry bytes, sizes, time left and per-episode pack progress. If the user prefers “of all missing episodes in the season” (not-yet-asked-for ones in M at 0 %), the page's own episode list gives that count; the figure then reads lower while part of a season is unasked, which is why the batch is the default.

Not proposed

A ring drawn on the cloud button itself: the cloud is an action and the ring is an indicator, and the header already badges a button (the played countdown) — two badges in one row read as noise. The figure in the misc line under the title: it is then far from the ring and not read as one thing. Percent digits inside the header ring: the ring is row-size (2.5em), where the rule keeps the glyph and puts the number in text. The series page can take the same indicator (total over all seasons, the same aggregate the series card uses) with no new work; not drawn here, say if wanted.
