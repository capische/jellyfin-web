Rows at three sizes: desktop · phone 360 · TV 125 %

The same rows at the three root sizes, whatever the device toggle says, so the digits can be judged where they are smallest. Digits are 0.8em of the row's 1em: 12.8 px on desktop, 11.5 px on the phone, 16 px on TV — all above the 11 px floor. Times: "12 min left", "1 h 05 min left"; stalled has no time; queued says how many are ahead; a pack episode's time comes from the pack's rate and that file's remaining bytes.
