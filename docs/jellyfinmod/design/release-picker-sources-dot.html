Picker sources: the dot, in each indexer's colour

Chosen direction (user, 2026-10-25): the plain icon with a dot. Each indexer gets a colour of its own, fixed and visible in settings; while the picker searches, the dot circles the icon and takes the colour of the indexer being asked; the checklist shows the colours. One thing to know first: today the torrent ring's segments are coloured by state (green answered, red failed, light blue asking, pale blue waiting), not by indexer, so an indexer colour is new. The proposal below gives every indexer one, uses it in the dot, in the checklist and in settings, and offers to recolour the torrent ring's segments by indexer as well so the two match.

The palette

Eight hues that stay apart from each other on the dark and the light theme and from the two colours that mean something already (the primary blue, the failure red). Assigned in the indexers' order the first time an indexer is seen, kept for its lifetime; a ninth indexer starts the palette again. Not editable, but shown wherever the indexer is named.

The dot, state by state

Searching, the train (user, 2026-10-25): when the search starts the dot leaves its corner and circles the icon once per 1.2 s as the lead, grey. Every indexer being asked is a follower in its own colour, trailing the lead on the same circle, 40° apart in the order they were asked (up to seven; on a 2.5em icon that is a short comet tail). When an indexer finishes (answered, no results or failed) its follower closes the gap, merges into the lead, and the lead takes that indexer's colour; the followers behind it close up by one place. So the lead always shows the last indexer that finished, and the tail shows how many are still out. Transform and opacity only. Reduced motion: no circling; a still blue dot in the corner while searching. Narrowed and quiet: a still grey dot, as before. The first strip loops a 9 s demo with three indexers; the frozen frames show two moments of it.

Finished — two ways to end

F1 · the lead comes to rest (recommended): when the last follower has merged, the lead slows into the corner and stays there in the colour of the last indexer that finished (the train's natural end); if any indexer failed it rests red instead. When the picker is reopened for a new search it is grey again. A quiet end that still says "done" and "who was last". F2 · the dot becomes the ring: on finishing, the corner shows a 0.9em ring of segments, one per ticked source in its colour, failed ones red, as a miniature of the torrent ring: at a glance how many sources answered and which failed, still, no text. It stays while the results are on screen. More to look at than the user asked for; drawn so the choice is real.

The checklist

Same checklist as before; the checkbox itself is the indexer's colour (filled when ticked, outlined in the colour when unticked; a dark tick on the two light hues), the name in the text colour, the state at the right in the secondary colour. (User, 2026-10-25: black text, coloured checkboxes instead of dots.)

The torrent ring, recoloured by indexer (to match)

Today's ring: a segment per indexer, coloured by state. Matched: each segment in its indexer's colour, the state carried by opacity (waiting 0.3, asking pulsing, answered full) and red only for a failure, so the ring, the dot and the checklist speak one colour language. The indexer lines under the ring take the same dot. Optional; without it the dot's colours mean nothing in the ring.

Settings · Indexers

The colour sits before the indexer's name on its row (and on the Dashboard page's indexer list), read-only, with the tooltip "Colour in the release picker". No field, no picker; it is there so a colour seen in the picker can be looked up.
